📖 League Rules

These rules are based on the spreadsheet you supplied and can be made fully editable from Commissioner Settings.

Draft: 4 rounds; each manager drafts 4 couples.

Draft Order: Randomly selected at the start of each round.

Wildcard: There are exactly 2 Wildcard picks available during the draft. A Wildcard counts as a normal roster spot.

Wildcard selection: After both Wildcard picks are drafted, 2 eligible couples are randomly selected and assigned to those Wildcard spots. A Wildcard couple cannot already be on that team (or drafted by anyone else).

Wildcard elimination: Once a Wildcard couple is eliminated, that Wildcard spot is gone. There is no replacement couple.

Judges: The four judges' scores are added together.

Perfect Score: A 40 receives an additional 10 fantasy points.
